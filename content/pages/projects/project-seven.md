## Sebbe Voice Cloning: Neural Voice Synthesis

This project showcases a practical implementation of voice cloning and speech synthesis. It provides tools to capture a short voice sample, adapt a pre-trained TTS model, and synthesize natural-sounding speech in the cloned voice.

### Key Features

- **Sample-based cloning**: Create a voice profile from a few seconds of audio
- **Text-to-Speech generation**: Convert arbitrary text into the cloned voice
- **Model adaptation**: Fine-tune or adapt pre-trained models for speaker style
- **Audio export**: Download synthesized speech as WAV/MP3 files

### Technical Highlights

- **Languages & Frameworks**: Python, PyTorch
- **Audio processing**: Mel-spectrogram conversion, noise reduction, silence trimming
- **TTS Models & Vocoders**: Uses modern TTS pipelines (converter + vocoder) and supports integration with popular open-source model checkpoints
- **Performance**: GPU acceleration for faster synthesis and model adaptation
- **Deployment**: Docker-ready for reproducible environments

### Ethics & Safety

Voice cloning can be misused; the project includes notes on ethical usage, consent, and practical safeguards to prevent abuse.

### Example Use Cases

- Accessibility: generate speech in a preferred voice for assistive technologies
- Prototyping: rapid voice prototyping for media and product demos
- Research: experimentation with speaker adaptation and audio quality
